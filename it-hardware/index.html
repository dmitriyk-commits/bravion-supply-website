Wholesale FAQ

What is your minimum order?
There is no fixed minimum. We quote from a single box up to full pallets; per-unit pricing improves with volume and repeat orders.
Do you sell to consumers?
No. We work with businesses only — resellers, system integrators, companies, institutions and online sellers.
Are your products new and genuine?
Yes. All product is new and factory-sealed, sourced through authorized U.S. distribution and retail channels. We do not sell refurbished or open-box items as new.
What payment methods do you accept?
Wire transfer and corporate card. Payment terms for repeat buyers are discussed individually with your purchasing manager.
Do you ship across the U.S. and internationally?
Yes. Domestic orders ship by ground or LTL freight. Export orders are consolidated at our hubs in Delaware, Florida or California and shipped by air or sea with full export documentation.
How do you quote prices?
Every request receives a written quote with current availability. Prices move with the market, so each quote states how long it is valid.
What about warranty?
Manufacturer warranty applies as issued by each brand. For product we supplied, we coordinate warranty and RMA claims on your behalf.
Can you assemble PCs for our office?
Yes — assembly to order through partners, on request. Tell us your headcount and tasks, and we will propose a configuration, quote the full setup and agree delivery and installation details.
