Product Range

Core Components

Orders are placed through authorized distribution and retail channels in the United States. We do not source from the gray market.

03

Consolidation & Export

Individual shipments are received and consolidated at our Delaware consolidation point, operated together with our forwarding partner. Commercial invoice, packing list and export documentation are prepared before departure.

04

Freight & Handover

Consolidated cargo moves by air or sea to Tashkent, where it is handed over for regional distribution. Tracking is provided at each stage.

Sourcing & Compliance

Where Our
Product Comes From

All IT hardware supplied by Bravion Supply LLC is purchased new and factory-sealed through authorized distribution and retail channels within the United States. We do not source from unauthorized or gray-market suppliers, and we do not supply refurbished, pulled or open-box product as new.

Manufacturer warranty terms apply as issued by the respective manufacturer. Warranty coverage, duration and regional validity vary by brand and by product, and are confirmed in writing at the time of quotation. Bravion Supply LLC handles claim coordination for product supplied by us within the terms applicable to that product.

Bravion Supply LLC is an independent importer and wholesale supplier. We are not an authorized representative, agent or official distributor of any hardware manufacturer, and we make no claim to represent any brand we supply.

Export shipments are prepared and documented in cooperation with a licensed U.S. freight forwarder in accordance with applicable United States export regulations.
