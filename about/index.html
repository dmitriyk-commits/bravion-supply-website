Our Story

From Florida
to the World

Bravion Supply LLC is a Florida-based importer and wholesale supplier working across several product lines, from building materials to IT hardware.

Our IT Hardware division is built on a simple idea: trade buyers should be able to source the full range of computer hardware from the U.S. through one accountable partner — with a manager who knows their order, consolidated shipping and documentation that holds up at every border.

We buy new, factory-sealed product through authorized U.S. channels and deliver it where our customers need it: across the United States and to export markets.
