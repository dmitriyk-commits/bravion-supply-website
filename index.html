Who We Are

Your Direct
Sourcing Partner

Bravion Supply LLC is a Florida-based direct importer and wholesale supplier. We connect buyers with competitive manufacturing, organize import logistics, and support dependable regional delivery.

We operate two product divisions: IT Hardware — computer components, systems and networking supplied wholesale across the U.S. and for export — and Building Materials, including our own BRAVION FLOORS line.

Our operating model is lean and practical: direct communication, carefully controlled overhead, clear specifications, and reliable execution from purchase order to delivery.
